Our Story

Rooted in Tradition, Refined by Passion

Mathura has always cooked for celebration. Pedas pressed from slow-reduced milk. Thandai ground by hand for Holi. Dals left on the fire until evening. Festival food, made with patience. Every family here keeps a recipe it will not share. At Adyra, we take that Braj tradition and refine it with fine-dining technique. The flavours stay familiar. The craft is new.
Our produce comes from farms around Mathura. Our milk, ghee and paneer come from local dairies. We cook with what they send us that week. The menu follows the season, nine courses served at an easy pace. And the room is made for long dinners: forty-eight seats, soft light, and no rush to turn the table. Stay for one more course. Stay for the conversation.
