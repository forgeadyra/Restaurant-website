Privacy Policy

Adyra Fine Dining is a concept restaurant. This website was built by AdyraForge as a portfolio piece. There is no real restaurant, and a reservation request sent through this site is not a booking.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into the reservation form:

  • your name, email address and phone number
  • the date, time and number of guests you choose
  • anything you write under special requests

Please don't include sensitive information, such as health details, beyond what's needed to describe a dietary requirement.

How the form works

The site is hosted on Netlify. The reservation form is handled by Netlify Forms, which stores each submission in our Netlify account. Netlify may process this data outside India. You can read Netlify's privacy policy.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your request. We don't sell or share them, add you to a mailing list, or use them for advertising. We delete submissions once they are no longer needed.

Other services

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when the page opens. The address links open Google Maps, which is covered by Google's own privacy policy. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email hello@adyrafinedining.com.
